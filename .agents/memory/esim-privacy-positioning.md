---
name: eSIM privacy positioning
description: Privacy and compliance constraints for the Wayline eSIM business
---

Wayline is intended to operate as an independent international prepaid eSIM service. Position it as privacy-conscious, not anonymous or untraceable. Support identity checks when required by local law, payment providers, or telecom suppliers; only issue phone numbers and network service through legitimate providers.

**Why:** The product brief explicitly rules out anonymity guarantees and fabricated telecom credentials.

**How to apply:** Use this boundary in marketing, checkout, activation, customer support, number services, and future provider integrations.
